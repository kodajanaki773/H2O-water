HOW IT WORKS

Four Water Treatment Methods

Different methods remove different impurities. The right treatment depends on the water source.

🫗

01. Sedimentation

Allowing heavier suspended particles to settle can help reduce visible dirt before further treatment.

🧪

02. Filtration

Suitable filters remove particles and, depending on the technology, some other contaminants.

♨️

03. Boiling

Bringing water to a rolling boil can kill many disease-causing germs. Follow local public-health guidance.

💧

04. Disinfection

Appropriate disinfection can inactivate harmful microorganisms when used correctly.

STAY WATER-SMART

Choose Safe Drinking Water

Water that looks clear is not necessarily safe to drink. Some contaminants cannot be seen, smelled, or tasted.

  • Follow local drinking-water safety advice.
  • Use a treatment method appropriate for the identified risks.
  • Store treated water in clean, covered containers.
  • Remember that boiling does not remove every chemical contaminant.
