WATER SOURCES

Where Does Water Come From?

Water exists in many forms throughout our environment.

Rivers & Lakes

Surface water supports ecosystems and can provide drinking water after appropriate treatment.

Groundwater

Water stored beneath the ground can be accessed through wells and springs.

Rainwater

Rain replenishes water sources. With suitable systems, it can also be collected for later use.

NATURE'S CYCLE

How the Water Cycle Works

1. Evaporation: The Sun warms water, turning some of it into water vapour.

2. Condensation: Water vapour cools and forms tiny droplets in clouds.

3. Precipitation: Water falls from clouds as rain, snow, or other forms of precipitation.

4. Collection: Water flows into rivers, lakes, and the ground, continuing the cycle.
